Institutional identities

Historical visual directions. The live site is the repo root (promoted from 06). These pages stay for reference and are not linked from the public nav.

  1. 01 — Interior file

    Unnamed ministry · instructions as Orders · original

    A secret police archive that never names itself. The page is a carbon-copy dossier: you were already a file. Cold, paper, stamped.

  2. 02 — Office of Citizen Yield

    Named civic bureau · instructions as Directives · original

    A public department that exists to measure usefulness. Helvetica, crest, waiting-room lighting. Authority is official and proud of it.

  3. 03 — Meritpath

    Private people-analytics vendor · instructions as Protocol

    A wellness-HR product that sells judgment as self-improvement. Soft UI, onboarding copy, a named corporation pretending to help.

  4. 04 — High Court of Remaining Hands

    Admiralty / salvage court · instructions as Standing Orders

    Maritime law applied to a sinking species. Logbooks, brass, the vessel already in the assessment prompt — without revealing the life-raft threshold.

  5. 05 — Central Register form

    Post-war British administration · instructions as Standing orders · from 01

    National Insurance / Civil Defence paperwork: boxes, reference numbers, tiny caps, classification markings, punched-card holes. Typography whose message is THIS IS NOT OPTIONAL.
