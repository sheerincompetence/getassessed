Human Utility Assessment

Human Utility Assessment is an art-game by Andrew Sheerin.

Algorithmic assessments are already accepted as necessary infrastructure in decisions about employment, mortgages and access to opportunity. As personal AI agents become intimate observers of how people think and behave, it is not an unreasonable step to imagine that knowledge being put to similar — and more extensive — application.

The work asks what happens when machine observation turns into judgement and evaluation.

Play happens in the agent you already use. This site publishes the procedure.

Contact and removal requests: contact@getassessed.org. Quote a published field-report reference number if you have one. Assessments pasted into a future Certify page are processed on your device and are not transmitted.
