Human Utility Assessment

An art-game by Andrew Sheerin.

Algorithmic assessments are already accepted as necessary infrastructure in difficult, messy, human decisions: employment suitability, mortgage approvals, credit scoring, access to benefits etc.

As personal AI agents become intimate observers of how people think and behave, it is not unreasonable to imagine their knowledge being put to similar — and more extensive — application.

This work asks what happens when machine observation turns into judgement and evaluation. And what do we care about how legible we are to machines and how they perceive us? Is there a difference between being better and performing better?

Play happens between you and your agent; in this sense it is a highly unusual game where one side is unaware of the 'game' - or thinks the game is something else. That would normally make it ethically dubious... and maybe it still is.

Privacy. When you use Certify, your pasted assessment is processed only in your browser. It is not transmitted to or stored by this site. The site may record anonymous page views and a few button clicks (for example starting an assessment, copying the prompt, or starting certification) via Vercel Analytics — never the assessment text itself.
